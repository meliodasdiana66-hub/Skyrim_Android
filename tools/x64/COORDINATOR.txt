--bind-iat is a separate opt-in. It initializes read-only IAT slots with guest tokens before guest_seal. Call tokens and trap tokens are addresses with the high bit set. They are not host pointers and the pages are not executable. A collision, a bad name, an unmapped slot, or a store failure restores every qword and leaves the image unbound. A second bind writes the same tokens. After the seal, another bind is refused. The default pe_data_map command does not pass --bind-iat.

A synthetic instruction list can call through one of those slots. It checks 16-byte alignment and the 32-byte shadow, then dispatches only a registered token. An illegal target, a trap token, a step budget, or a depth budget is a named failure and does not invent a success. The comparison is the virtual shim, not Unicorn and not a Windows DLL. The image entry point is not fetched. The RCX graph contract was not changed.
